Grimm, V., Railsback, S. F., DeAngelis, D. L., and others. (2020). The ODD protocol for describing agent-based and other simulation models: A second update to improve clarity, replication, and structural realism. *Journal of Artificial Societies and Social Simulation*, 23(2), 7. https://doi.org/10.18564/jasss.4259

## NetLogo

Wilensky, U. (1999). *NetLogo*. Center for Connected Learning and Computer-Based Modeling, Northwestern University, Evanston, IL. https://ccl.northwestern.edu/netlogo/

## Scope note

These references describe methodological foundations and the modeling platform. They do not validate the empirical assumptions or parameter values of the present model.
